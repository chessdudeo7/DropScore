    Under the hood

    How DropScore reads a video

    Four stages, all classical computer vision. No model to download, no cloud, and nothing it cannot explain.

    01

    Find the keyboard

    Locate the keybed strip, then fit the 88-key grid from the black-key pattern so every x-position maps to a pitch.

    02

    Track the tiles

    Segment falling rectangles by colour and motion, follow them frame to frame, and read their height as note length.

    03

    Convert to time

    Measure the fall speed in pixels per second and turn each strike-line crossing into a precise note onset.

    04

    Engrave

    Quantize onsets to a detected tempo grid, split hands, spell accidentals against the key, and emit MIDI and MusicXML.
