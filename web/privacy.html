What happens to your video

The video you upload is used only to transcribe it. It is deleted as soon as the transcription finishes, whether it succeeds, fails or is cancelled. It is not watched by anyone, shared, or used to train anything.

What is kept

  • The results (MIDI, MusicXML, PDF and a list of notes) are kept so you can download them. They are deleted automatically 24 hours after the job finishes, once 50 newer jobs have run, or when the server restarts, whichever comes first.
  • Results are reachable only through a long random link. Anyone you share that link with can download them.

What is not collected

  • No account, name or email is needed.
  • No cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Your IP address is held in memory while your videos are being transcribed, only to limit how many one visitor can run at once. It is not written to disk or logged.

Your responsibility

Upload only videos you have the right to use. Transcriptions of copyrighted music are for your personal study.
